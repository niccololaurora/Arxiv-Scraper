What this does

It takes the topics you defined in the Scanner and asks OpenAlex — 167 million papers, all fields, not just arXiv — two questions about each pair of them: how many papers talk about both, and how many you would expect if the two subjects were unrelated.

A pair that comes back far below its expected number is one the literature has not connected. That is a candidate, not a discovery: most thin intersections are thin because they make no sense. The ones worth your time are the pairs where both halves are large and growing, the overlap is small, and what little exists is recent — so the table shows all of that, and a link to read the papers that do exist before you get excited.

Everything is counted within this corpus. Leave it at quantum and a pair is compared against quantum physics; empty it and it is compared against all 167 million papers, where any two quantum subjects look tightly linked simply because they are both quantum.

A project of the Pure and Applied Quantum Mechanics Group — Department of Physics, University of Milan.
